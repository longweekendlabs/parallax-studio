What it does

One focused workflow instead of a giant editing suite.

Auto Depth

Depth Anything V2 separates a subject from its background, then the map is feathered where it would otherwise tear: a hard outline does not stretch under displacement, it folds back over itself.

Body Relief

For photographs of people. Finds every figure and its pose, then rebuilds the depth inside each one so chest, waist, hips and legs each carry their own parallax instead of the figure sliding as one cutout.

Depth paint

Or do it yourself. Brush size, hardness, strength and depth value over a colour-ramped overlay that actually shows what you have painted, with undo and redo.

Built for

Artists who want motion from still art without a 3D pipeline.

Visual novel scenes

Layered character art and backgrounds stay editable while you tune depth for subtle motion.

Short loops

Export GIF or MP4 loops for previews, social posts, trailers, or in-game presentation.

Offline by design

The depth, segmentation and pose models download once and then stay on your machine. No cloud service ever sees your pictures.
